## You can get started now

Residual has no free tier, and 1.0 shipped with nothing to subscribe to. In 1.0.1 the paywall works: start the free trial or pick a plan, and you're in. Anything already on your phone stays as it was.

## Help that stays out of your way

- Nothing dims or covers your day any more. The coach cards, the tours and the "New here?" banners are gone.
- More → Help is a list of questions, like "Why did my meals change size?" and "Why is my meal locked?", each answered on one short page. The practice day is there too, with less reading up front.
- Pantry, Meals, Recipes, Daily targets and Foods to avoid each end with a link to their page.
- If you're still on the sample numbers, or have fewer than three pantry foods, one line under the ring says so. Tap × and it's gone.

## Getting set up

- After the practice day, setup asks which foods you usually keep, so your first plan starts from them. You can skip it.
- Plan my real day opens the plan form instead of planning straight away.
- Suggest for me keeps the numbers it calculated. If you used it in 1.0, check Daily targets: you may have the starting 2,700 kcal.

## Fixed

- Typing an amount replaces the number. It could add to it before (130 became 220130). Clearing a target no longer leaves its last digit behind.
- Plan with AI says how many planned meals it will replace before it does. Accept ignores taps for a second, so a plan can't be accepted by accident.
- A day you're still building stays calm. The ring no longer turns amber after breakfast, and the sentence under it says what's left. Amber is for a day that's over, or a past day that came up short.
- Landing exactly on the edge of a range counts as in range, and the differences shown match the numbers beside them.
- Recalibrate can't leave a row further from its target than the plan had it.

Something wrong? [Support](/support/) reaches us.
