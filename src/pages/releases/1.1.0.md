## If you use Residual on more than one iPhone

Update each of them. An iPhone still on 1.0.1 keeps syncing and your data is safe, but it doesn't know about servings: it shows a food in servings in grams, at the same weight, and a re-size there can leave it on part of a serving. A meal you share with someone on 1.0.1 reaches them in grams too, at the same amount.

## Size a food by the serving

A beef stick is 32 g. No plan should ask for 50 g of one, and until now Residual would.

- Open a food in your pantry and turn on Size by the serving. It uses the serving size from the label, so fill that in first.
- From then on, plans and re-sizes use whole servings of it: one stick or two. That holds at every portion rounding, Exact included.
- In a meal, the unit menu offers Servings wherever Residual knows what a serving weighs. You can set a half by hand, and it stays.
- Foods already in your meals stay as they are until you change one. If you change the serving size, every meal you haven't logged follows, and so do your saved meals.
- Recipes stay in grams. Weigh what you take.

## Try a food before you apply

Close the residual and Recalibrate now have Add a food under each meal that's still open. Before, seeing what one more food would do meant leaving the review, adding it to the meal and starting over.

- Choose a food finds one in food search.
- Suggest from my pantry offers up to three foods you have in stock that land the day closest. Foods to avoid are left out.

The food is sized with the rest of the day, so you see where the day lands with it before you decide. Nothing changes until you tap Apply.

## Scanning, and the pantry

- The barcode scanner shows four brackets where to hold the barcode, and reads only a code inside them, so a second barcode on the box isn't picked up.
- Add a food your pantry already has, by scanning it or from search, and you get that food instead of a second copy. It opens saying it's already in your pantry, and if you'd marked it out of stock, it's back in stock.
- A food that's new to your pantry says it was added. Before, adding one looked the same as adding one you already had.

## A log you're in the middle of stays put

You'd start logging a meal, weigh a few things, put the phone down, and come back to the plan's amounts. Now what you enter stays until you tap Log meal, whether you swipe the sheet away, lock the phone or quit the app.

- Only the foods you changed hold their amount. The rest follow the plan, so if the meals ahead get re-sized while you're mid-meal, the foods you haven't touched move with them.
- Nothing counts toward your day until Log meal. Cancel asks before it throws away what you entered.
- On Today, swiping a meal you're partway through says Log meal, and logs what you entered.

## Off plan, and when it matters

- A meal that drifted by an amount you can see says Off plan, then the amount. Some meals showed a bare "+14 kcal" before. A drift too small to show says nothing.
- When the calories didn't move, Off plan names the row that did: "Off plan · +1 g protein".
- The meal's line turns amber only when that meal alone is past one of your day's ranges. Eggs a few grams heavy stay green.

## In range means the same thing everywhere

Today's ring, the bars under it, a meal's sheet, Close the residual and the widget each judged the edge of a range a little differently, so one could call a row in range while another called it off. They share one rule now.

- A gap under half a unit reads ±0.
- The widget uses Today's colors and words, so a number that's in range but not on target is no longer green there.
- Your targets list in the order you set them everywhere: the hero bars, the review, Suggest a fix, the widget, the share card and the meal sheet.
- A range typed as 0 becomes the smallest range, ±5 kcal or ±0.5 g, and the field shows it. It no longer marks every small difference in a meal as off plan.

## A food you set in a review stays put

Keep a food or set its amount in Close the residual and Apply, and it stays at that amount until you change it, re-plan, log the meal, or a later review moves it. The next review shows it as "Kept where you put it". A food you add in a review lands at the amount the review showed.

## Other changes

- Recipes has its own tab: Today, Meals, Recipes, Pantry, More. The Recipes rows on Meals and in More are gone.
- Open a saved meal on the Meals tab and tap Reorder to put its foods in your order. Add it to a day and they arrive in that order.
- More → AI planning → Delete recent plan requests clears the plan requests kept on your iPhone. They're left out of your backups now, too.
- Where a food's numbers come from reads Saved food or AI estimate. Two screens said "Saved" and "Estimate".
- The keyboard's button says Update and sits centered, and the field you're typing in moves up so the button doesn't cover it.
- A food in a recipe or a saved meal shows the same lines as a food in a meal: protein, carbs, fat and fiber with the calories in bold, what the source says the calories are when that differs, and where the numbers come from.
- A new icon: one ring, with what you've eaten, three dots for what's planned, and a dot where the day lands. The share cards, the paywall and the update screen use it too.

## Fixed

- Deleting a saved meal could crash the app.
- More days land in range: the planner looks further before giving up on a row, and lands each row within the range as the app shows it, so a row no longer stops at +75.8 and shows +76.
- A small portion, like a few grams of oil, could drop to 0 g. Portions in grams land on whole grams.
- A food you set above 450 g, like a big bowl of soup, was cut back to 450 g when the day needed more food.
- Recalibrate is offered only when a row shows off target, and no longer leaves a row further off on a day that was already in range.
- If your day changes while a review is open, the review closes and says so. Apply from an out-of-date review could put a food in the wrong meal or change one you'd logged.
- Return on a cleared number field puts its number back. With a hardware keyboard or the keyboard minimized, a number field still shows Update.
- Close the residual takes taps half a second after it opens instead of a full second.
- Removing an ingredient from a recipe could crash the app, most often right after adding another. Saved meals had the same weak spot. Both are fixed.
- Undo, after removing a food inside a recipe, a saved meal or a meal, was drawn behind the sheet where you couldn't tap it. It shows over the sheet now. Other messages were hidden the same way, like the one that says a scanned barcode isn't in the food database.
- With portions set to Exact, Close the residual could leave calories a few kcal outside its range to land a later target. It keeps the order you set now.
- Daily targets cut Calories and Protein short to make room for the arrows. The names show in full now, with the arrows at the right.
- At the largest text sizes, the date above the ring wraps to a second line instead of being cut off.
- Deleting a meal or a note at just the wrong moment could crash the app.
- Tapping Suggest a fix twice quickly could stop the spinner while the second search was still running.
- A shared food's name could carry characters that don't show, so it read as something it wasn't. They're removed when a share arrives.
- A new install whose first save failed could be left with an empty food library for good.
- Accepting a plan or applying a fix no longer holds up the screen while the portions are sized.
