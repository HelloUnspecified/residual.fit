## Not your doctor or dietitian

Residual doesn't give medical or nutrition advice, and it doesn't diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.

## Numbers are estimates

Food data, AI suggestions, and the portions Residual works out can be wrong. Check labels and amounts yourself.

## Allergies and conditions

Residual and its AI don't know your medical history or allergies. Make sure every food is safe for you.

## Ask a professional first

Talk to your doctor or a registered dietitian before changing how you eat, especially if you're pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, have diabetes, kidney disease, an eating disorder, or another condition, or take medication affected by diet.

## If you feel unwell

Stop and get medical help. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

## You're responsible for what you eat

You decide what to eat and how much, and you use Residual at your own risk.

## In the app

Residual asks you to agree to this before you start: by continuing, you agree that Residual isn't medical advice and that you're responsible for what you eat. You can read it again any time in More → Health disclaimer.
