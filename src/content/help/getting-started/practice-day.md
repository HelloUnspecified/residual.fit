## Where it is

You're offered it during setup, after your targets: tap **Try a practice day**. After that, it's at `More → Help → Practice day`, under "Try it".

A banner at the top reads **Practice day**, with "Nothing here is saved or counted." under it. The day starts with a copy of your targets, your pantry, the food library and your foods to avoid. Nothing you do in it syncs, exports or reaches your history, and closing it throws it away.

## What you'll do

The card at the bottom is headed "Your first day" and counts the steps. Some steps only explain what's on screen; tap **Next** to move on. The others wait for you to do something, and show what to tap.

1. **Let's plan a practice day.** What's ahead, in one line.
2. **Calories, start to finish.** The ring is today's calories. The green band is your range and the tick at the top is your target.
3. **Tap the ring, hold to share.** Tapping the ring changes what it shows. Holding the day shares it as an image.
4. **One button, four things.** The `+` at the bottom holds a meal, a note, a plan written by AI, and, when the day is off, a suggested fix. The pill beside it names your next step.
5. **Plan a meal.** Tap `+`, then **Add a meal**, and add a food or two to breakfast.
6. **Planned, not eaten.** The ring and the bars move to show your plan. Nothing counts as eaten until you log it.
7. **Plan the rest of your day.** Tap **Plan the rest**. On the practice day a set plan adds lunch, dinner and a snack, so there's no waiting on AI.
8. **Your day lands in range.** The arc ends inside the green band.
9. **Log breakfast.** Swipe Breakfast right, then tap **Log as planned**.
10. **Eaten fills the ring.** Solid green is what you've eaten; the lighter arc is still ahead.
11. **Now real life happens.** Lunch came with more rice. Tap Lunch, raise the rice to the amount the card gives, then tap **Log meal**.
12. **Close the residual.** Tap **Close the residual**, look over the changes, then tap **Apply**.
13. **That's Residual.** Plan, eat, log, and close the residual whenever the day drifts.

On a step that does something, **Show me** does it for you. **Skip step** moves past it.

> [!NOTE] Lunch isn't off enough yet
>
> If you don't raise the rice far enough, the day can still absorb it, and the card says "Not off course yet". Logging locked Lunch, so tap it, tap **Unlock to edit**, and raise the rice again.

## Move around or leave

The `…` on the card goes **Back** a step, skips with **Skip this step**, or ends with **End the guide**.

To leave, tap **Close** at the top. Residual asks "Leave the practice day?". **Leave** ends it; **Keep practicing** takes you back. You can come back to it any time from `More → Help`.

## After the practice day

During setup, the practice day leads into the rest of it: "What do you keep?" if your pantry is empty, then **Plan my real day**. See [Set up Residual](/help/getting-started/set-up/). Taken from `More → Help`, it closes back to where you were.

For what each part of Today means, see [Read the Today screen](/help/today/today-screen/). For the step that matters most, see [Close the residual](/help/closing-the-residual/close-the-residual/).
