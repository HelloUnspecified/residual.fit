## Before you start

Residual needs an iPhone with iOS 27 or later. It's iPhone only, and there is no Android version. You'll also need to be 18 or older.

## The first run, step by step

1. Read the three intro pages and tap **Continue** on each, then **Let's get started**. **Skip** goes straight to the agreements.
2. Confirm your age. The screen is titled "For adults 18 and older". Tap **Confirm my age** and Apple answers with an age range, never your birthday. If Apple doesn't share one, tap **I'm 18 or older**.
3. Read the **Terms of Use** and tap **Agree**.
4. Read the health disclaimer, "Not medical advice", and tap **I understand and agree**.
5. Start your Residual Plus free trial, unless you already subscribe. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, and you can restore a subscription you already have from the same screen. [Residual Plus](/help/data-and-settings/residual-plus/) explains the plans, and [pricing](/pricing/) shows what they cost.
6. Set your targets. Choose **I have my numbers** to type the ones a coach or dietitian gave you, or **Suggest for me** to work them out from your body, activity and goal. Every number stays editable.
7. Tap **Try a practice day**, or **Skip the practice** to go straight to Today.
8. After the practice day, Residual asks "What do you keep?". Tap **Choose my foods** to pick a few foods you usually have, so your first plan starts from them, or **Skip for now**. You only see this step if your pantry is empty.
9. Tap **Plan my real day**. The plan form opens for today.

> [!NOTE] If you skipped the intro
>
> **Skip** on the intro pages takes you through the agreements and the trial, then straight to Today, on sample numbers. A line under the ring reads "You're on sample numbers." Tap **Set yours ›** to replace them, or open `More → Daily targets` any time.

## Setting your targets

On **I have my numbers**, type calories, protein, carbs and fat, each with a range. Landing anywhere inside a range counts.

On **Suggest for me**, the calorie calculator works out all four targets from your body, your activity and your goal. If you switch back to **I have my numbers**, what you typed there is still there.

A calorie target under 1,200 kcal a day needs you to confirm a doctor or dietitian set it before you can go on. More on targets, ranges and their order is in [Set your daily targets](/help/targets/daily-targets/).

## The practice day

The practice day is a guided day on a copy of your targets and foods. You plan a meal, let Residual plan the rest, log breakfast, go off plan at lunch, and close the residual. Nothing in it is saved. It takes a few minutes, and you can take it again from `More → Help` whenever you like. See [Take the practice day](/help/getting-started/practice-day/).

## When you see these screens again

The age check, the Terms and the health disclaimer come back only when a newer version needs agreeing to. They appear the next time you open the app, before anything else.

## After setup

Today opens with your first plan, or with "Nothing planned yet." if you skipped it. [Read the Today screen](/help/today/today-screen/) explains what's on it. To plan from there, see [Plan a day with AI](/help/planning/plan-a-day/), or [Add meals, foods and notes yourself](/help/planning/build-a-day/).
