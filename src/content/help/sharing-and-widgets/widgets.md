## Add one

1. Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
2. Tap **Edit**, then **Add Widget**.
3. Find Residual in the list and tap it.
4. Swipe to the widget and size you want, then tap **Add Widget**.

For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap **Customize**, choose the Lock Screen, and tap the widget area under the time. Landing and Eaten are the two with Lock Screen sizes.

## The six widgets

| Widget | What it shows | Sizes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landing | Will today land in range? Calories and macros, zoomed to your range, and what's next. | Small, medium, large; Lock Screen circle, rectangle and inline |
| Clock | Your day on a 24-hour dial: when you ate, what's planned, and how long it's been. | Small, large |
| Next Up | The next meal to eat, its numbers, and where the day stands. | Medium, large |
| Eaten | Calories and macros eaten so far, against what's planned. | Small, medium; Lock Screen circle |
| Left | How many calories you have left today. | Small |
| Plan | The day's meals around the ring, with the next one picked out. | Small |

Every widget shows today. They update as you plan and log in the app.

## Tapping a widget

Tapping a widget opens Residual on Today. Some widgets also have a button for the next step:

- **Log** and the next meal's name, such as Log lunch, opens that meal ready to log. It never logs anything by itself, so you still check the amounts and tap **Log meal** in the app.
- **Plan today**, when nothing is planned yet, opens Today so you can plan it.

How logging works from there is in [Log a meal as planned](/help/logging/log-as-planned/).

## “Open Residual to plan today.”

A widget says this when Residual hasn't given it a day to show yet, and while the app is locked behind Residual Plus. Open Residual and the widget fills in with today, even before anything is planned. If the app is locked, see [Locked out, or asked to update](/help/troubleshooting/locked-or-update-required/).
