## Share your day as an image

1. On Today, press and hold the ring. The Share your day sheet opens.
2. Swipe through the four designs and stop on the one you want.
3. Tap **Share** and pick where it goes.

| Design | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Story | Full screen, for Instagram and other stories |
| Square | Square, for a feed post |
| Sticker | Transparent, to place over your own photo |
| Numbers | Every target: eaten, planned, and where it lands |

The image shows the ring on the gauge it's showing, so tap the ring first if you want a different view. Before anything is planned or logged there's nothing to show, and Today says “Plan or log a meal, then long-press here to share your day.” On a past day with nothing logged, it says there's nothing to share.

## Share a meal, a recipe or a food

Anything you can keep in Residual can be sent to someone else. Each share opens the iPhone's share sheet with a link, so you can send it in Messages, Mail or anywhere else, or by AirDrop.

| To share | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| A meal on Today | Press and hold it, then tap **Share**. In the open meal, it's the share button at the top. |
| A saved meal | On the Meals tab, swipe it right or press and hold it. |
| A recipe | On the Recipes tab, swipe it right or press and hold it, or use the share button in the open recipe. |
| A pantry food | On the Pantry tab, swipe it right or press and hold it. |
| A food in a meal | In the open meal, swipe the food right and tap **Share**. |
| A food in your library | In `More → Food library`, swipe it right or press and hold it. |

A meal goes with its foods, their amounts and your daily calorie target, so it can be sized for whoever opens it. A recipe goes with its ingredients.

## Open something shared with you

Tap the link, or accept the AirDrop. Residual opens a sheet titled Shared with you, with everything in the share and where each food's numbers come from. Someone without Residual who taps the link sees a page on residual.fit with **Open in Residual**.

What you can do depends on what was sent:

- A meal: **Save as a favorite meal** keeps it on Meals. **Add to days…** puts it on any days you pick, as a plan. Nothing counts until you log what you ate, and your solver fine-tunes it with the rest of your day.
- A recipe: **Save to your recipes**. Add it to any meal from Add food, under Recipes, by weight.
- A food: **Add to pantry**, or **Save as a favorite**.

**Not now**, or swiping the sheet down, closes it without keeping anything.

## Sized for you

When the person who sent a meal plans for a different number of calories than you do, the sheet offers two ways to take it: **As shared**, their portions unchanged, or **Sized for me**, with a percentage. Sized for me scales every portion by your daily calories against theirs, kept between half and double. The line under the choice says what it means, for example “81% of their portions, from your daily calories (2100 vs their 2600).”

When the two calorie targets are the same, or one isn't known, the meal comes as shared and there's no choice to make.

## When a share won't open

- “That share didn't come through. Ask for it to be sent again.” The copy was gone before Residual could read it. Ask for it again.
- “This isn't a Residual share, or it was damaged on the way.” The link or file isn't complete. Ask for it to be sent again.
- “This was shared from a newer version of Residual. Update the app to open it.” Update Residual in the App Store, then open the share again.
- “There's nothing in this share.” It was sent empty. Ask the sender to share it again.
