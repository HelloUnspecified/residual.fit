## Swipe to log

On Today, find the meal under YOUR DAY. Swipe it to the right and tap **Log as planned**.

The meal moves up with the other meals you've eaten, its line turns solid green, and a lock shows beside its calories. The ring and the bars fill by what it added. Logging locks a meal so what you ate stays what you ate. [Locked meals and days](/help/logging/locked-meals-and-days/) explains how to change one later.

Swipe the other way for **Copy** and **Remove**.

## Or use the button at the bottom

When the day lands in range, the button at the bottom of Today names the next meal to eat, such as **Log lunch**.

![Residual's Today screen. Breakfast is logged with a lock beside 465 kcal. Lunch, Snack and Dinner are planned, each with a dashed line. The sentence under the ring reads Lands in range. Three meals ahead, sized to fit. The button at the bottom reads Log lunch.](shot:01-today "The day lands in range, so the button offers the next meal: Log lunch.")

1. Tap **Log lunch** (or whichever meal it names). The meal opens, ready to log, with every amount filled in from the plan.
2. Check it against what you ate. If anything differed, change it here; [Log what you actually ate](/help/logging/log-what-you-ate/) shows how.
3. Tap **Log meal** at the bottom. The button shows the meal's calories, for example **Log meal · 562 kcal**.

Residual says "Logged Lunch." and takes you back to Today. Tapping the meal itself opens the same screen.

> [!NOTE] When the swipe says Log meal
>
> If you opened a meal and changed an amount without logging it, Residual keeps what you entered. The swipe then reads **Log meal** instead of **Log as planned**, and it logs what you entered, with the plan for every food you didn't touch.

## What logging records

Each food is logged at its planned amount. A food matched to a real food in the plan carries that match into the log. A food that is still an AI estimate is logged as one; when you log from the meal, the screen says how many foods will log on the AI estimate. [Match AI estimates to real foods](/help/your-food/match-foods/) covers matching.

The meal also takes the time you logged it. You can change it later under **Eaten at** in the meal.

## Is logging a chore?

It shouldn't be. Most meals eaten as planned are one swipe. When something differed, open the meal and change only that food; its amount steps by your portion rounding, 5 g unless you've changed it. New foods come from food search, a barcode, or a photo of the Nutrition Facts label, read on your iPhone. See [Find a food](/help/your-food/find-a-food/).
