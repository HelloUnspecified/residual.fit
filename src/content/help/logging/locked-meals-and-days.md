## How to tell

A logged meal shows a lock beside its calories on Today. Open it and the header reads **Logged · locked**. Its amounts show without **+** or **−**, and the only button at the bottom is **Unlock to edit**.

A locked day shows a lock where **Reorder** usually sits, beside YOUR DAY. The sentence under the ring ends "Day locked".

## Unlock a meal

1. On Today, swipe the meal to the right and tap **Unlock**. The meal opens, unlocked. Or open the meal and tap **Unlock to edit**.
2. Change what you need. The header reads **Logged · unlocked**. Changes here are to what you ate; the plan stays as it was.
3. Tap **Log meal** to save it and lock it again. On a past day the button reads **Log meal and lock**.

On a past day, a meal you leave unlocked locks again when you close it. Today's meals stay unlocked until you log them again, and the swipe on Today then offers **Lock**.

## Remove a logged meal, or move it back to planned

Both are in the meal once it's unlocked.

- **Move back to planned** drops what you logged and turns the meal back into a plan. "Moving back to planned drops what you logged; the plan's matches stay."
- **Remove meal** takes the meal off the day. Today offers **Undo** for a few seconds.

## Why a day locks

A day locks when every meal on it is logged and there's nothing left to do: always on a past day, and on today or a day ahead when it's in range or over. A day that's still short stays open, so you can log as you go without unlocking it for each meal.

On a locked day, the `+` at the bottom offers **Unlock the day** in place of **Add a meal**, and leaves out **Plan with AI** and **Suggest a fix**.

## Unlock the day

1. On Today, tap the `+` at the bottom.
2. Tap **Unlock the day**. Residual says "Day unlocked. Add what you need."
3. Add the meal or the food you forgot, and log it.

The day locks again on its own the next time you log a meal, or the next time Residual starts. To lock it yourself, tap the `+` and **Lock the day**.

> [!NOTE] Unlocking changes nothing on its own
>
> Unlocking a meal or a day only lets you edit. Nothing re-sizes and nothing is logged until you tap **Log meal**. If a change moves the day off, Today offers **Close the residual**, which re-sizes only meals you haven't eaten. See [Close the residual](/help/closing-the-residual/close-the-residual/).
