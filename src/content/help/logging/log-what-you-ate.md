## Open the meal

On Today, tap the meal. It opens ready to log, with the header **Logging** and every amount under What you ate filled in from the plan. It works the same on a past day. A meal on a day ahead opens its plan instead, since there's nothing to log yet.

Nothing you change here counts until you tap **Log meal**.

## Change what differed

1. Find the food that was different. Tap **+** or **−** to change its amount, or tap the number and type it.
2. To use another unit, tap the unit beside the number and pick one under **Measure in**: grams or ounces, cups, spoons and milliliters for a food with a known density, and **Servings** for a food with a known serving weight.
3. If you skipped a planned food, swipe it left and tap **Didn't eat**. It stays in the meal with nothing eaten, so the change shows.
4. If you ate something extra, tap **Add what you ate** and find it. [Find a food](/help/your-food/find-a-food/) covers search, barcodes and labels.
5. Tap **Log meal** at the bottom. It shows what you're logging, for example **Log meal · 609 kcal**.

The **+** and **−** move by your portion rounding, 5 g unless you've changed it under `More → Daily targets`. A food measured in Servings moves a whole serving at a time.

## What you see while you log

As soon as you change something, the header reads **Logging · in progress**. The summary at the top compares the plan with what you've entered.

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Plan | What the meal was planned to give. |
| Ate | What you've entered. |
| Drift | The difference. A row is amber when its drift alone is past that target's range for the day. |
| The line under it | Once the meal differs from its plan: **Off plan · inside the day's bands** for a small difference, or **Off plan · past a band of the day** for one past a range. |

Beside each food you changed, the planned amount shows in amber, for example "plan 150 g".

> [!NOTE] What you enter is kept
>
> You can swipe the meal away, lock your phone or quit Residual before you log. What you entered is still there when you come back, and the swipe on Today reads **Log meal**. Only **Cancel** throws it away: it asks "Discard what you entered?", and **Discard** puts the meal back to its plan.

## On Today

A meal that differed from its plan shows a chip with the difference, for example **Off plan · +237 kcal**. Its line turns amber when the difference is past a range of the day, and stays green when it isn't.

If what you ate moves the day out of range, the button at the bottom becomes **Close the residual**. That re-sizes the meals you haven't eaten. See [Close the residual](/help/closing-the-residual/close-the-residual/).

## Log something you didn't plan

1. On Today, tap the `+` at the bottom and choose **Add a meal**.
2. Tap **Add food** and add what you ate. Name the meal if you like; otherwise it's named from its foods.
3. Tap **Log what you ate instead ›** under the name, check the amounts, and tap **Log meal**.

## Change the plan instead

While you log, **Edit the plan instead ›** under the meal's name switches to the plan, and what you entered is kept. In the plan, **Log what you ate instead ›** brings you back. Once a meal is logged, it says "The plan stays as it was." Changes there change what you ate, never the plan.

## Do I need a food scale?

No. A scale is the most accurate, but cups, spoons and ounces work, and close is good enough inside a range. [Amounts are weights](/help/reference/how-the-numbers-work/#amounts-are-weights) explains why.
