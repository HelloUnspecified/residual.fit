## Where it is

Open `More → Foods to avoid`. The plan sheet has the same list one tap away: when you plan a day, the **Foods to avoid** row under "Anything to include or avoid?" opens it and shows what's on it, such as "Almonds, shellfish and 2 more".

## Add a food

1. Under Add a food, type the food in the field that reads "A food, like almonds or mushrooms".
2. Under it, choose **Allergy** or **Don't eat**.
3. Tap **Add**.

The food goes on the list under Never plan. An allergy shows a red warning mark beside it; a food you don't eat shows a thumbs-down. Add the same food again and Residual changes its reason instead of listing it twice.

## Common allergies

Under Common allergies, one tap adds an allergen as an allergy: Peanuts, Tree nuts, Dairy, Eggs, Gluten, Soy, Fish, Shellfish and Sesame. Each one covers its whole family. Tree nuts covers almonds, cashews, walnuts and the rest; Dairy covers milk, cheese, yogurt and butter. Once you've added one, it leaves the row of buttons.

## How a food is matched

Residual matches by the food's name, word by word. A word covers every food with it in the name, so "almonds" covers almond butter too, and it never catches a word that only contains it: almond doesn't rule out salmon.

An allergen family knows a few things that only look like members. Dairy doesn't rule out peanut butter, almond milk or coconut milk, for example.

> [!NOTE] Check the label
>
> Residual can only see a food's name, not every ingredient in it. A sauce or a packaged food can hold something the name doesn't mention. Always check the label, especially for an allergy.

## What it keeps out

Every planner is told the list: Claude, Apple Intelligence, and the plans built from your own foods. Whatever comes back is checked against it before you see it, and a food on the list is left out. The same check runs on Suggest a fix, and on **Suggest from my pantry** when you add a food in [Close the residual](/help/closing-the-residual/close-the-residual/).

The list doesn't touch what's already there. A meal you planned or logged by hand keeps its foods, and you can still add any food to a meal yourself.

## Change or remove one

- To change why a food is on the list, tap **Allergy** or **Don't eat** beside it and pick the other.
- To remove one, swipe it left and tap **Delete**, or tap **Edit** at the top.

More on planning a day, and the request box where you can rule out a food for one day only, is in [Plan a day with AI](/help/planning/plan-a-day/).
