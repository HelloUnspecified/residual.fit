## Save a meal from Today

1. On Today, open a meal you'd eat again.
2. Scroll down and tap **Save as favorite**. It changes to **Saved to favorites**.

The saved meal keeps the foods at the amounts the meal has now: what you ate if it's logged, the plan if it isn't. It shows on the **Meals** tab.

## Build one from scratch

1. Open the **Meals** tab and tap **Add a meal** at the bottom.
2. Type a **Meal name**. A **Note** is optional.
3. Tap **Add food** and add each food. Set each amount with **+** and **−**, or type it.
4. Tap **Done**. A meal with no name asks for one first and offers a name made from its foods.

A new meal with no foods isn't kept. With no saved meals yet, the Meals tab reads "No saved meals yet" and can add an example to show you how one looks.

## Add a saved meal to a day

- **On the Meals tab:** tap **Add to today** under the meal. It adds the meal, planned, to the day Today is showing, so on another day it reads **Add to tomorrow** or the date. Residual offers **Undo** for a few seconds.
- **To several days:** open the saved meal and tap **Add to other days…**. Pick days over the next two weeks, or **Every day**, **Weekdays** or **Weekends**, then tap **Add to 5 days** with your count. Each day gets the meal as planned, at the end of the day. Nothing is logged.
- **From inside a meal:** tap **Add food** in a meal. Your saved meals are listed first, under Favorite meals. Picking one "Adds every food in the meal, at its saved amount."

Once a saved meal is on a day, Close the residual can re-size that meal like any other. The saved meal keeps its own amounts.

## Edit a saved meal

Tap a saved meal on the Meals tab to open it.

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Meal name, Note | What it's called and a line about it. |
| Where it's from | **Source (a cookbook, a chef, a friend)** and **Website**, so you can find the recipe again. |
| The summary | Calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber at these amounts. |
| Foods | Each food and its amount. Swipe one left to remove it. |
| **Reorder** | Shows with two foods or more. Drag the foods into the order you make and log them. You can also press and hold a food and drag it. |
| **Add food** | Adds another food. |
| **Remove favorite** | Deletes the saved meal. "Meals already added to a day keep their copy." |

The order you set is the order the foods appear in when you add the meal to a day.

When you close it, meals you added from it that are still ahead, today or later and not logged, take your changes. A copy you changed by hand on a day is left as it is, and Residual says so. Logged meals never change.

## Favorite foods

You can save a single food as well. In a meal, swipe the food right and tap **Favorite**. Favorite foods are listed on the Meals tab under Favorite foods, and in every food search under Favorites. Swipe one left on the Meals tab to remove it.

## How the planner uses them

When Claude or Apple Intelligence plans a day, it can reach for your saved meals. [Suggest a fix](/help/closing-the-residual/when-the-day-cant-land/) can offer to add one when the day is short.

A dish you cook from weighed ingredients, where you eat a portion of the whole, is better as a recipe. See [Make a recipe](/help/your-food/recipes/).
