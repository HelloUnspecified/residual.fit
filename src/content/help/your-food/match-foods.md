## What "to match" means

When Claude or Apple Intelligence plans a day, it names foods and amounts. A food it took from your pantry, your saved meals or foods you've used already has real numbers. A food it named on its own carries the planner's estimate of its nutrition, marked **AI estimate**.

Estimates are close, but they're guesses. Matching links the food to its USDA entry, or to a food of your own, so the plan and the log use real numbers. Nothing is matched behind your back: you pick each match.

## Where you'll see it

- **On Today:** under the ring, a line such as "2 foods are AI estimates" with **Match** beside it. A meal row says how many of its foods are still to match, for example "2 to match".
- **In a meal:** each food has a line under it saying where its numbers come from. An estimate reads "AI estimate — tap to match the real food".
- **In a plan you're reviewing:** "All 15 foods are AI estimates — match them after you accept." Or, when most came from your own foods, how many matched and that the rest are estimates.

## Match one food

1. Open the meal and tap the food's line, "AI estimate — tap to match the real food".
2. Search opens with the food's name filled in. Pick the right entry: your own foods first, then USDA.

The line then reads "Matched · USDA", with the brand when it has one. Tap it again any time to pick a different match.

## Match several at once

Tap **Match** under the ring on Today, or **Match 3 foods to USDA** in a meal you're planning. **Match foods** opens with a row for each estimate.

1. Each row shows the food's numbers now and the closest USDA food by name, with its numbers per 100 g. A suggestion starts switched on only when its calories agree with the estimate.
2. Check each one is what you'll eat. Tap **Change…** to pick another, or switch it off to leave the estimate. A row that found nothing says "No close match found." and offers **Search…**.
3. Tap **Match 3 foods** at the bottom (with your own count).

Matching swaps an estimate for USDA's numbers, so the day's totals can change. If that moves the day off, Today offers **Close the residual**.

Your pantry has the same thing: **Match 4 items to USDA** at the top of the Pantry tab matches pantry foods that aren't matched yet, and the match stays on each item.

## What matching carries

- A match made in the plan carries into the log when you log the meal.
- A match is kept for next time, so the same food in later plans uses it.
- A food that's still an estimate when you log it logs as an estimate. The meal says how many will, before you tap **Log meal**.

## Where a food's numbers come from

Every food shows where its numbers come from.

| Label | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Matched · USDA | From USDA FoodData Central. Search rows say "USDA generic" or "USDA branded". |
| Manually verified | Read from the package's own label by your phone, and checked by you. |
| Matched USDA · User override | A USDA food you corrected from your package. Your numbers win; the USDA match is kept. |
| Saved food | A food saved or entered by hand. |
| Library | One of Residual's built-in foods, with typical numbers. |
| Recipe | One of your recipes, worked out from its ingredients. |
| AI estimate | A planner's guess. Match it to a real food so the day is sized on real numbers. |

Residual explains Matched · USDA, Manually verified, the override and AI estimate under `More → Help → How are the numbers worked out?`. To add a food that isn't in USDA at all, read it from its label: see [Find a food](/help/your-food/find-a-food/).
