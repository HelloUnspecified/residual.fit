## A recipe or a saved meal?

A saved meal is a set of foods eaten together at fixed amounts. A recipe is one food made of others: you cook a pot and eat some of it. If you'd weigh your portion out of the whole, make a recipe. If you'd eat every food at its own amount, save a meal; see [Save meals you eat often](/help/your-food/saved-meals/).

## Make one

1. Open the **Recipes** tab and tap **Add a recipe** at the bottom.
2. Type a **Recipe name**. **Note (how you make it)** is optional, and so is **Where it's from**: a source and a website.
3. Under Ingredients, tap **Add ingredient** and add each food at the weight that went in. "Weigh each as it goes in. The dish's numbers follow from these amounts."
4. Under The dish, enter the **Finished weight** if you cooked it. Leave it blank for something that weighs what went in, like a salad.
5. Use the **Makes 1 serving** stepper to say how many servings the dish makes, up to 50.
6. Tap **Done**. A recipe needs a name to be kept.

## Weigh the finished dish

Cooking drives off water, so a stew weighs less than its raw ingredients. The nutrients are still all there, in fewer grams. Weigh the finished dish and Residual divides by that weight, so each 100 g counts for what it really holds. Once you've entered it, the note under it says how much went in, how much came out, and what that is as a percentage.

Leave it blank and Residual uses the total weight of the ingredients.

## What it adds up to

Under What it adds up to, the recipe shows three lines:

| Line | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Whole recipe | Everything in the dish, and its finished weight. |
| Per 100 g | What a meal carries. |
| Per serving | The finished weight divided by the servings it makes. Shows when it makes more than one. |

"A meal carries the per-100 g numbers; the amount you add (or eat) is grams of the dish."

## Add it to a meal

1. In a meal on Today, tap **Add food** (or **Add what you ate** while logging).
2. Find the recipe under Recipes, or type its name.
3. It starts at one serving, or at the amount you usually eat once you've had it a few times. Change the grams to what you'll eat.

Residual sizes a recipe like any other food when it plans or closes the residual. Recipes stay in grams. Weigh what you took.

A recipe shows in search once it has a name, at least one ingredient, and a weight. A finished weight far below what went in, so low the numbers can't be right, keeps it out of search until you fix it.

## Change or remove one

Open the recipe from the Recipes tab and change anything. When you close it, meals ahead that hold it, today or later and not logged, take the new numbers. Logged meals keep theirs.

**Remove recipe** at the bottom deletes it. "Meals that hold it keep its numbers."

If an ingredient is still an AI estimate, the recipe's row on the Recipes tab says how many, and so does the recipe, because the dish's numbers are estimates too. Remove that ingredient and add it again from the food database or a label. [Find a food](/help/your-food/find-a-food/) covers both.
