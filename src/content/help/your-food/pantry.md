## Start your pantry

Open the **Pantry** tab. An empty pantry reads "Your pantry is empty", with a button to choose from a list of common foods. Tap the ones you keep, then the button at the bottom, which counts them: **Add 12 foods**.

To add one food at a time, tap **Add a food** at the bottom. Search opens; pick the food, or scan its barcode. [Find a food](/help/your-food/find-a-food/) covers search and labels.

You can also add a food from elsewhere: swipe a food right in a meal and tap **Pantry**, or swipe a food right in `More → Food library`.

A protein, a carb and something green give the planner plenty to work with.

## In stock and out of stock

Each food has an **In stock** switch at the right of its row.

- **On:** the food is on the shelf. Plans can use it.
- **Off:** the row grays out and reads "Out of stock · plans skip it".

Swipe a food right for the same switch, or press and hold it for **Mark out of stock**. When you've restocked, tap the `…` at the top and **Mark everything in stock**. The same menu has **Add common foods**.

The buttons at the top filter the list: **All**, **In Stock** and **Out of Stock**.

## How the pantry shapes your plans

- The planner prefers your pantry when it builds a day, and skips anything out of stock.
- **Only plan from my pantry**, on the plan form, keeps a plan to the foods you have in stock. See [Plan a day with AI](/help/planning/plan-a-day/).
- In Close the residual, **Suggest from my pantry** tries your in-stock foods in a meal and lists those that land the day closest.
- **Suggest a fix** looks in your pantry first.

## Fix a food's numbers

Tap a food to open **Edit food**. It shows where the numbers come from, with **Match to USDA…** to link it to a USDA food (or **Match to a different USDA food…** when it already is). Change anything to match your package and tap **Save**. A USDA food you change becomes your override, and the USDA match is kept.

When several foods aren't matched, **Match 4 items to USDA** (with your count) sits at the top of the list. "A match is kept on the item, so meals and plans use the real numbers from then on." [Match AI estimates to real foods](/help/your-food/match-foods/) explains matching.

To remove a food from your pantry, swipe it left and tap **Delete**.

## Size a food by the serving

Some food comes in pieces. A beef stick is 32 g, and no plan should ask for 50 g of one. Tell Residual it comes in servings.

1. On the Pantry tab, tap the food.
2. Check **Serving size** under Nutrition Facts, per serving. The switch needs it.
3. Turn on **Size by the serving**. The note under it reads "Residual plans and re-sizes this in whole servings of 32 g. Use it for food that comes in pieces, like a bar or a beef stick."
4. Tap **Save**.

Without a serving size, the note says "Add the serving size above to size this by the serving."

From then on, plans use whole servings of the food, and Close the residual moves it a whole serving at a time or not at all, whatever your portion rounding. The other foods take up the rest.

> [!NOTE] Foods already in your meals
>
> Turning the switch on changes the pantry food, not the meals that already hold it. In a meal, tap the unit beside a food's amount and pick **Servings** to change one yourself. The **+** and **−** then move a whole serving, and you can still type a half; Residual keeps it.

If you change the serving size later, every meal you haven't logged follows, and so do your saved meals. Logged meals keep what you ate. Recipes stay in grams: weigh what you took.
