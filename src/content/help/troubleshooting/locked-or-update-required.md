## The app shows only Residual Plus

When your subscription or free trial ends, Residual locks. It opens to the Residual Plus screen, with no **Not now** and no way to the tabs behind it, More included. Nothing is deleted. Your days, foods and targets stay on your iPhone and in iCloud, and the app opens as before once you subscribe again.

From the lock you can:

- Subscribe again: pick a plan and tap its button. See [Start your free trial and manage Residual Plus](/help/data-and-settings/residual-plus/).
- Restore a subscription you already have: tap the “…” button at the top, then **Restore purchases**.
- Take your data with you: tap the “…” button, then **Export my data**. It opens the same export as `More → Export to a spreadsheet`, which the lock keeps you from. How it works is in [Sync, export and delete your data](/help/data-and-settings/sync-export-delete/).

While the app is locked, Residual's widgets say “Open Residual to plan today.”

## “No subscription to restore on this Apple Account.”

Restore purchases asked the App Store and found no Residual Plus on the Apple Account this iPhone is signed in to.

1. Check which Apple Account you're signed in with in Settings. It has to be the one you subscribed with.
2. In Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and look for Residual. If it's not there, or it has expired, there's nothing to restore.
3. If it's there and active, tap **Restore purchases** again.

If it still says this, write to support@residual.fit.

## “Couldn't reach the App Store to restore. Try again in a moment.”

The App Store didn't answer. Check your connection, wait a moment and tap **Restore purchases** again.

## “You're offline” or “The App Store isn't answering”

Residual Plus and its free trial load from the App Store. When they can't, the screen says one of these instead of showing the plans:

- “You're offline”: “Residual Plus and its free trial load from the App Store. Connect, then try again — or restore a subscription you already have.”
- “The App Store isn't answering”: “Residual Plus and its free trial couldn't be loaded right now. Try again in a moment, or restore a subscription you already have.”

The screen keeps every way out:

- **Try again** asks the App Store again.
- **Restore purchases** restores a subscription you already have.
- **Export my data**, when the app is locked, exports everything.
- **Email support** opens an email to us with your app version and iPhone model filled in.

## A newer version is out

When a newer Residual is in the App Store, a sheet suggests it, at most once a week. It says what the update is, may link **See what's new**, and has **Update in the App Store** and **Not now**. Not now closes it until next week. While you're behind, `More` has a row to update too.

## “Update Residual to keep going”

When your version no longer works, Residual shows only an update screen. Its title and message can name the update; when they don't, the title is the one above. Update is the one way on: tap **Update in the App Store**, install it, and open Residual again. Your days, foods and plans stay as they are.

**Export my data** on the same screen exports everything first, if you want a copy.
