## What works offline

- Your targets and every day you've planned.
- Logging, as planned or what you actually ate.
- Close the residual, Recalibrate and Suggest a fix. The solver runs on your iPhone.
- The ring, the gauges and the sentence that says where the day lands.
- Food search for the foods you've saved, used or put in your pantry, plus USDA's generic foods, which come with the app.
- Reading a Nutrition Facts label, which happens on your iPhone.
- Planning, with Apple Intelligence on iPhones that have it turned on, or from your pantry, saved meals and built-in foods.

## What waits for a connection

- Planning with Claude, and asking Claude for a food in Close the residual.
- Packaged foods from USDA, and barcodes you haven't saved before.
- iCloud sync. Your changes are kept on the iPhone and catch up when you're back online.
- Starting a free trial, subscribing and restoring a subscription.
- Signing up for the newsletter.

## “Offline · a plan is built from your pantry on this iPhone.”

Today shows this under “Nothing planned yet.” when you're offline, and the button reads **Plan from your pantry**. Tap it and the day is planned without Claude: by Apple Intelligence where it's on, otherwise from your own foods. Once you're connected, the button goes back to **Plan today**.

## “You're offline, so …”

If you plan with Claude, a plan made while you were offline says so at the top. The rest of the line says who planned it instead. Accept it, or connect and plan again for a plan by Claude. More in [A plan isn't by Claude, or planning didn't work](/help/troubleshooting/planning-problems/).

## Food search offline

Search shows “You're offline. Showing your foods and generic foods; USDA search returns when you're back online.” A barcode you haven't saved before goes straight to **Add from the label**, where you can type the numbers from the package. More in [Food search, barcodes and labels](/help/troubleshooting/food-search-problems/).

## Residual Plus offline

The Residual Plus screen says “You're offline. Connect to start your free trial or restore your subscription.” If the app is locked and can't reach the App Store, it says “You're offline”, with **Try again**, **Restore purchases** and **Export my data**. Your export works without a connection. See [Locked out, or asked to update](/help/troubleshooting/locked-or-update-required/).

## Residual says you're offline, but you're not

Residual goes by what your iPhone reports about its connection. Check that Wi-Fi or cellular data is on and that Airplane Mode is off, then try again. The message clears by itself once the connection is back.
