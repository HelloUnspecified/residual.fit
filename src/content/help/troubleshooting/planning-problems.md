## Who planned the day

The plan review shows its planner at the top:

- “Planned by Claude”
- “Planned by Apple Intelligence on this iPhone”, when Claude was off or couldn't be reached and Apple Intelligence is on your iPhone.
- “Built from your pantry and library”, when neither could plan. With **Only plan from my pantry** on, it reads “Built from your pantry”.

How to choose, and what each one sends, is in [Choose who plans](/help/planning/who-plans/).

## The plan says Apple Intelligence while you're online

When Claude didn't plan the day, a line on the plan says why. Each one ends with who planned instead.

- “Claude isn't set up in this build, so …” This copy of Residual has no way to reach Claude. If you see it in the App Store version, write to support.
- “You've reached today's Claude limit, so … Claude is back tomorrow.” Claude has a daily limit. Plan with the one you have, or wait until tomorrow.
- “You're offline, so …” Connect and plan again. See [Using Residual offline](/help/troubleshooting/offline/).
- “Claude couldn't plan this day, so …” Claude couldn't be reached, or ran into an error. Try again in a minute.
- “Claude's answer couldn't be used, so …” Claude answered with nothing Residual could use. Try again, or change your request.
- “Claude didn't plan this day, so …” Residual couldn't tell why. Try again in a minute.

No line at all means you have Claude turned off. Check `More → AI planning`: the row shows who plans for you, and **Plan with Claude** turns it on.

## “expect rough portions”

“This day was built from your pantry and the food library — expect rough portions.” Neither Claude nor Apple Intelligence planned it, so the meals were put together from your own foods. The solver still sizes every portion, but the mix of foods may not be what you'd choose. Edit any meal after you accept, or turn on Claude or Apple Intelligence and plan again.

A plan built this way can't be refined. The review says “Refining needs Claude or Apple's on-device model. This plan was built from your foods; edit any meal after you accept it.”

## “Planning didn't work”

“Couldn't build a plan right now. Check your connection and try again — or add meals yourself.” No planner came back with a plan. Check your connection and tap the plan button again. You can always build the day by hand: tap the `+` at the bottom of Today, then **Add a meal**. See [Add meals, foods and notes yourself](/help/planning/build-a-day/).

## “Your plan hasn't changed”

You asked to refine a meal or the whole plan, and nothing came back. The plan on screen stays as it was, and the message says why:

- “You've reached today's Claude limit. Claude is back tomorrow, or edit any meal after you accept this plan.”
- “You're offline. Try again when you're connected, or edit any meal after you accept this plan.”
- “Claude couldn't make that change. Try again, or edit any meal after you accept this plan.”
- “That change couldn't be made. Try again, or edit any meal after you accept this plan.”

Accepting the plan as it is and changing a meal afterwards always works.

## Asking Claude for a food in Close the residual

When your own foods can't close the day, the review offers **Ask Claude for something to add**. If it can't help, the line under it says why:

- “Your amounts changed while Claude answered. Ask again.” You moved a food while waiting. Tap it again.
- “Claude suggested …, but it doesn't bring the day closer.” The food it picked didn't help. Try **Add a food** yourself.
- “Claude didn't find a food that fits. Add one to a meal yourself, or apply and see more ways.”
- “You've used today's Claude requests. Add a food yourself, or ask again tomorrow.”
- “You're offline. Connect to ask Claude.”
- “Couldn't reach Claude. Try again in a moment.”

More ways out are in [When the day can't land](/help/closing-the-residual/when-the-day-cant-land/).
