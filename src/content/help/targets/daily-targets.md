## Where they are

Open `More → Daily targets`. Changes save as you type. They apply to today and every day after it, planned or not. Past days keep the targets they had.

## Each target

Daily targets has a row for each of calories, protein, carbs and fat. A row holds:

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| The number | The target: kilocalories for calories, grams for the rest. |
| ± range | How far either side still counts. Landing anywhere inside the range is a hit. |
| The arrows | Move the row up or down to change the order it lands in. |
| Floats | Aim for this target last. A miss never marks your day off. |

Under the rows, a line adds up what your macros come to in calories. A check means they agree with your calorie target. If they don't, it says by how much and which target gives first.

## The landing order

Rows land in the order they're listed, top to bottom, and each row shows its place. When the meals can't land every target, the later ones give first. The usual order is calories, protein, carbs, then fat.

1. Find the target you care about more, say fat over carbs.
2. Tap its up arrow until it sits where you want it.

A floating row always lands after the rows that don't float, so the arrows move a floating row only among the other floating ones.

## Let a target float

Tap **Floats** on a row to make it the lowest priority. Residual still aims for it and gets it as close as it can after the others, but a miss on it never marks your day off. Tap **Floats** again to make it a target like the rest.

At least one target has to stay fixed, so the last row that doesn't float can't be turned into one that does.

> [!NOTE] Below 1,200 kcal
>
> A calorie target under 1,200 kcal a day isn't generally advised without a doctor's supervision. Residual asks you to confirm with **A doctor or dietitian set this number** before it uses it. Leave without confirming and your last targets stay.

## Work them out from your body

No numbers from a coach or dietitian? Tap **Calculate from your body**. The calorie calculator works out calories and macros from your age, height, weight, activity and goal. Tap **Use these numbers** to fill in your targets, or **Cancel** to leave them as they were.

## How portions round

Under Portions, **Round portions to** sets the grid planned portions snap to: Exact, 1 g, 5 g or 10 g. It's 5 g unless you change it. A food sized by the serving always moves a whole serving at a time, whatever this is set to.

## When a change leaves a plan off

New numbers can leave a day you've already planned outside its ranges. Daily targets lists those days under Plans to close. Tap one to open it on Today and close the residual there. Meals you've eaten stay as logged. More in [Close the residual](/help/closing-the-residual/close-the-residual/).
