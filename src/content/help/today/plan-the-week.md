## Move between days

Swipe left or right across the day on Today to go to the next or the previous day. The title says which day you're on, and the first tab reads **Day** instead of **Today**.

To jump further, tap the circle with the date at the top right, or press and hold the title. "Go to a day" opens a month calendar. A green dot marks a day with something eaten, and a second color marks a day that's planned but not yet eaten; the legend under the calendar names them **Eaten** and **Planned**. Tap a day to go there. **Today**, at the top left, goes back to today, and **Done** closes the calendar.

On any day but today, a **Today** button sits beside the date circle. Tapping the first tab again does the same.

A day ahead works like today: plan it with the button at the bottom, which reads **Plan** and the weekday, or build it yourself with `+`. See [Plan a day with AI](/help/planning/plan-a-day/) and [Add meals, foods and notes yourself](/help/planning/build-a-day/).

## Copy a meal to other days

1. On Today, swipe the meal left and tap **Copy**.
2. The sheet "Copy" and the meal's name lists today and the 13 days after it. Each row says what that day already holds, such as "3 meals · 1,820 kcal" or "Nothing planned yet", and says so if the day already has this meal.
3. Tap the days you want, or tap **Every day**, **Weekdays** or **Weekends** to pick them all at once. **Clear** unpicks them.
4. Tap the button at the bottom. It reads **Pick the days** until you pick one, then **Copy to** and the day or the number of days.

The meal goes onto each day as a planned meal, at the end of the day. Nothing is logged. A message at the bottom says where it went, such as "Copied Breakfast to 5 days"; tap **Show** to go to the first one.

A saved meal can be added to several days the same way, from its sheet on the Meals tab. See [Save meals you eat often](/help/your-food/saved-meals/).

## Copy a whole day

Go to a past day. Once there's nothing left to log on it, the button at the bottom reads **Copy this day**. Tap it and pick days the same way. Each meal in the day goes onto each day you pick as a planned meal. The message reads "Copied this day to…" with **Show**.

> [!NOTE] Copies are plans, not logs
>
> A copied meal arrives planned, at its original amounts. When that day comes, log it as you eat it.

## The grocery list

Open `More → Grocery list`. It adds up every food in every meal planned from today on, and groups them by aisle in store order: Produce, Meat, Seafood, Dairy & eggs, Bakery, Grains & pasta, Pantry, Frozen, Drinks, then Other.

Each item shows the total amount, in grams (mL for a drink) with the US measure under it: ounces or pounds, or fluid ounces or cups, and how many meals it's in over which days, such as "2 meals · Fri–Sat". Meals you've eaten and days that have passed drop off. With nothing planned ahead, the list reads "Nothing planned ahead".

- Tap an item to check it off. It stays on the list, crossed through, so you can see what's done.
- If more of a food is planned after you checked it off, the item says how much more.
- A food you size by the serving shows how many servings to buy instead of the US measure.
- **Uncheck all** clears every check.
- The share button at the top right sends the list as text, to Notes, Messages or anywhere else.

Your check-offs are kept on this iPhone. The list itself comes from your planned days, so it changes whenever a plan does.
