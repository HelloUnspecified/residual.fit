## The header

At the top is the date, with the title **Today** under it. On any other day the title is that day ("Yesterday", "Tomorrow", or a weekday), and the first tab reads **Day** instead of **Today**.

The circle on the right shows the date; tap it for a calendar. Moving between days is in [Plan days ahead](/help/today/plan-the-week/).

## The ring and the bars

The ring is your calories. The bars beside it are protein, carbs and fat. On each bar, the solid part is what you've eaten and the lighter part is what's still planned. The shaded stretch is your range and the tick is your target. Landing anywhere inside the range counts.

A dot marks where the day will land if you eat the plan: green in range, amber or red when it's off.

Under the bars, **Fiber** shows the day's fiber, such as "31 g · 2 unknown". "Unknown" counts foods whose source gave no fiber. Fiber is shown for reference; it isn't a target, and nothing is balanced on it.

## The gauges

Tap the ring or the bars to change what they show. Each gauge answers one question:

| Gauge | The question it answers |
| --- | --- |
| Eaten | How much have I eaten? |
| Landing | Will I land in my range? |
| Left | How much do I have left? |
| Plan | What's the plan, and what's next? |
| Clock | When did I eat? |

The word under the ring names the gauge you're on. To keep only some of them, open `More → Gauges` and turn the others off. One always stays on.

Press and hold the ring to share your day as an image. See [Share your day](/help/sharing-and-widgets/share/).

## The sentence

Under the ring and bars, one sentence says where the day lands. The first part is the verdict, in green when the day is in range and in amber or red when it's off. The rest says why.

If some planned foods are still AI estimates, a **Match** link follows the sentence. See [Match AI estimates to real foods](/help/your-food/match-foods/).

## The button at the bottom

The wide button at the bottom always names your next step, and it changes with the sentence. The `+` at its left end holds everything else (see below).

| The sentence starts | What it means | The button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nothing planned yet. | The day is empty. | **Plan today**, or the weekday on a day ahead. Offline, **Plan from your pantry**. |
| N kcal still to plan. | The day is short and there's room for another meal. | **Plan the rest** |
| Lands in range. | Eat the plan and every target lands. | **Log** and the next meal, such as **Log lunch** |
| Plan lands over. / Plan lands short. | Nothing is eaten yet, and the plan misses a range. | **Close the residual**, "Size the N meals to fit" |
| A target and how far off, such as "Calories over 175." | Something you logged pushed the day out of range. | **Close the residual**, "Re-size the N meals ahead" |
| The same, then "Dinner alone can't fully recover." | The meals ahead can't absorb it within their limits. | **Close the residual** in red, "Re-size the last meal" |
| You changed your numbers. | New targets leave this plan off. | **Close the residual**, or **Plan the rest** |
| Day done. | Every meal is logged and every target is in range. | **Plan tomorrow** |
| Planning your day. | A planner is working. | **Planning…**; the × beside it stops the planner |

On a past day the button logs a meal you haven't, or offers **Copy this day**. "As close as it gets" after the verdict means the meals ahead have already been sized as far as they can go.

Amber and red on Today mean the day won't land in range as it stands. See [Close the residual](/help/closing-the-residual/close-the-residual/), and [When the day can't land](/help/closing-the-residual/when-the-day-cant-land/) for red.

## YOUR DAY

Your meals and notes, in order. A planned meal has a gray dashed line at its left; a logged meal has a solid line and a lock beside its calories. Each row shows its calories, its protein, carbs and fat, and its time.

A logged meal that differs from its plan shows a chip such as "Off plan · +237 kcal". Its line stays green while the difference is inside the day's ranges, and turns amber once it's past one. A planned meal can also show "N to match" when some of its foods are AI estimates, or "Getting thin" when it has been re-sized to under half its planned calories.

Tap a meal to open it. Swipe a planned meal right to log it as planned, or left to copy or remove it. Swipe a logged meal right to unlock it. **Reorder** on the header lets you drag meals into a new order. With nothing planned, the card reads "No meals yet", or "No meals logged" on a past day.

## The line under the sentence

While you're new, one line under the sentence helps with setup, one thing at a time. It goes away once it's done, or tap its × to hide it:

- "You're on sample numbers." **Set yours ›** opens your targets.
- "Plans start from the foods you keep." **Add a few ›** opens the Pantry tab.
- "Tap the ring to see where you'll land. Hold it to share."

## The + menu

Tap `+` at the left end of the bottom button. What it offers depends on the day:

| Item | When it's there | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add a meal | The day isn't locked. | Opens a new meal to plan. See [Add meals, foods and notes yourself](/help/planning/build-a-day/). |
| Add a note | Always. | A note on the day, with a title. |
| Plan with AI | Nothing is planned, or the day is short. | Opens the plan form. See [Plan a day with AI](/help/planning/plan-a-day/). |
| Suggest a fix | The day is off, and a fix could help. | The best few changes from foods you have. |
| Recalibrate | The day is in range, with meals ahead, and could land closer. | Small changes that land it closer. |
| Unlock the day | The day is done and locked. | Takes the place of **Add a meal**. |
| Lock the day | You unlocked a finished day. | Locks it again. |

On a past day with nothing left to do, the bottom button reads "Add a meal or a note" and the whole button is the `+`. Locking is covered in [Why a meal or day is locked](/help/logging/locked-meals-and-days/).
