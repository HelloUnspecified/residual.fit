## Why re-sizing has a limit

Close the residual keeps your foods and changes their amounts. Each portion stays between about half and double what was planned, so you never get a token portion or a meal you wouldn't eat. When one meal is left and the gap is bigger than that meal can take within those limits, re-sizing can't close it. [Close the residual](/help/closing-the-residual/close-the-residual/) covers the review itself.

## "Dinner alone can't fully recover."

> [!OFF] Dinner alone can't fully recover
>
> The day is off, and only one meal is left to absorb it. Even at half or double its plan, that meal can't bring every target back inside its range.

You see this after logging something that moved the day off, with a single meal still ahead. Today names the meal; with none named, it reads "The last meal can't fully recover." The button at the bottom turns red and reads **Close the residual**, **Re-size the last meal**.

Tap it anyway. Residual gets the day as close as it can, and the review shows where it lands before you apply anything. Then try one of the ways out below.

## "As close as it gets"

After you've closed the residual, a day that's still off keeps saying what's out, such as "Calories over 175.", followed by "As close as it gets". Residual has done what re-sizing can do, so the button moves on to the next meal to log.

If you tap **Close the residual** when there's nothing left to move, Residual says "Nothing to change: this is as close as these meals can get."

## "Getting thin"

A planned meal can show "Getting thin — under half the planned 640 kcal" (with its own number). Re-sizing has taken it below half of what it was meant to be. It's a cue, not an error: you may want to move a food into it, or plan it again.

## Ways out

### Add a food in the review

Under each meal in the review, **Add a food** tries one more food there and shows where the day lands with it. **Choose a food** opens food search. **Suggest from my pantry** lists up to three foods from your pantry that land the day closest, closest first. Only pantry items in stock are tried, and none on your [Foods to avoid](/help/your-food/foods-to-avoid/). If none helps, it says "Nothing in your pantry lands the day closer than it lands now."

When the amounts alone can't close the day, the review also looks through your foods on its own ("Looking through your foods for something to add…") and adds one to a meal if it lands the day closer. You can change its amount or skip it.

### Ask Claude

If your own foods still can't close it and the day is short, the review offers **Ask Claude for something to add**. Claude picks a food for a meal ahead; Residual sizes it, and you can skip it. It needs a connection, and the first time it asks before anything is sent. Claude has a daily limit; once you've reached it, the review says "You've used today's Claude requests. Add a food yourself, or ask again tomorrow." [Who plans](/help/planning/who-plans/) covers Claude and your privacy.

### Apply and look for more

**Apply and find a food for the rest** applies the amounts in the review, then opens Suggested fixes for whatever is left.

### Suggest a fix

Once the day has been re-sized and is still off, tap the `+` at the bottom of Today and choose **Suggest a fix**. Suggested fixes lists the best few changes from foods and meals you already have: your pantry, your saved meals and foods, your recipes, and foods you've eaten recently. Nothing on your Foods to avoid is used. Each one says what it does and where the day lands after it.

| A fix reads like | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Add 170 g Greek yogurt to Snack | Adds a food to a meal ahead. |
| Add a snack: 30 g almonds | Adds a new meal with one food. |
| Add Overnight oats · 447 kcal | Adds one of your saved meals. |
| Swap white rice for 150 g quinoa in Dinner | Replaces a food you haven't eaten yet. |
| Leave granola out of Snack | Takes a food out of a meal ahead. |
| Skip Snack | Removes a meal you haven't eaten. |

Under each, "Lands every target" or the target still off, such as "Calories 60 over". Tap one and Residual makes the change and re-sizes the day; nothing more to review. A food or meal it removes comes with **Undo**.

When the day is well short on calories, the first option is **Plan the rest of my day**: "New meals around what you've eaten, from what you have". It opens the plan form for the rest of today.

If nothing you have closes the gap, the sheet says "No fix from what you have". Add a food to a meal yourself, then close the residual again.

## When to let it be

Over on calories, adding food only adds to it, so the ways out are smaller portions, a swap, or leaving something out. Sometimes the honest answer is a day that lands a little off. A floating target never marks the day off, and every other target counts as a hit anywhere inside its range. [Set your daily targets](/help/targets/daily-targets/) explains ranges and floating.
