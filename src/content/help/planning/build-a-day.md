## Add a meal

1. On Today, tap `+` at the left of the bottom button, then **Add a meal**.
2. The meal opens with "Planning" at the top. Type a **Meal name**, and a **Description or goal (optional)** if you like. Leave them blank and Residual names the meal from its foods; type your own and it stays.
3. Tap **Add food** at the bottom, find the food, and add it. Search, barcodes and labels are in [Find a food](/help/your-food/find-a-food/).
4. Set each food's amount with its minus and plus buttons, or tap the number and type one. Tap **Update** above the keyboard when you're done typing.
5. Add the rest of the meal's foods the same way, then tap **Done**.

The meal goes onto the day as a planned meal. Nothing is eaten until you log it. The ring and the sentence on Today update to show where the day now lands.

> [!NOTE] Planned amounts get sized
>
> If the day goes out of range once you've added a meal, Today says so and offers **Close the residual**, which sizes the meals you haven't eaten to land it. The amounts you typed are where it starts from. See [Close the residual](/help/closing-the-residual/close-the-residual/).

## Set a time

Tap **Add a time** in the meal. It becomes **Planned for**, with a time you can change. The time shows on the meal's row on Today and on the Clock gauge. The × beside it removes the time.

## Amounts and units

The minus and plus buttons step by your portion rounding, 5 g unless you've changed it under `More → Daily targets`.

Tap the unit beside a food's amount to measure that food differently, such as cups for milk. A drink, or a food whose density is known, can be shown in cups, tablespoons, teaspoons, milliliters or fluid ounces. A food with a known serving weight can also be shown in **Servings**, and then steps a whole serving at a time. Whatever the unit, the amount is kept as a weight. See [How calories, portions and re-sizing are worked out](/help/reference/how-the-numbers-work/).

## Change a meal's foods

Swipe a food in the meal:

| Swipe | Button | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left | **Remove** | Takes the food out of the meal. |
| Right | **Favorite** | Saves the food as a favorite. |
| Right | **Pantry** | Adds the food to your pantry. |
| Right | **Share** | Sends the food as a link. |

A planned food that's still an AI estimate shows "AI estimate — tap to match the real food". When two or more are, **Match N foods to USDA** under the foods matches them in one go. See [Match AI estimates to real foods](/help/your-food/match-foods/).

At the bottom of the meal, **Save as favorite** saves the whole meal to the Meals tab, and **Remove meal** takes it off the day. See [Save meals you eat often](/help/your-food/saved-meals/).

## Change a planned meal

Tap a meal on Today to open it. On today, a meal you haven't eaten opens ready to log. To change the plan instead, tap **Edit the plan instead ›**. On a day ahead it opens on the plan. Logging is in [Log what you actually ate](/help/logging/log-what-you-ate/).

## Add a note

Tap `+`, then **Add a note**. The "Note" sheet has a **Title** and space to "Write anything about your day". Leave the title blank and it's taken from the note. Tap **Done**.

The note sits among the day's meals. To delete it, open it and tap the trash can. "Deleted the note" appears at the bottom with **Undo**.

A meal can have a note of its own too: open the meal and tap **Add a note** under "Note".

## Reorder the day

Tap **Reorder** beside YOUR DAY, drag the meals and notes into the order you want, then tap **Done**. You can also press and hold a meal and drag it. With VoiceOver, each row offers **Move up** and **Move down**.

A locked day can't be reordered: a lock takes the place of **Reorder**. See [Why a meal or day is locked](/help/logging/locked-meals-and-days/).

## Remove a meal

On Today, swipe a planned meal left and tap **Remove**. "Removed" and the meal's name appear at the bottom with **Undo**. A logged meal can't be swiped away: open it, unlock it, and use **Remove meal**.

To copy a meal to other days instead, see [Plan days ahead, copy meals and shop the list](/help/today/plan-the-week/).
