## Start a plan

On Today, tap the button at the bottom when it reads **Plan today** (or the weekday, on a day ahead). When part of the day is already planned or eaten and there's room for more, it reads **Plan the rest**. You can also tap `+`, then **Plan with AI**.

The plan form opens, titled "Plan my day", or "Plan the rest of the day".

## Fill in the plan form

1. Set **Meals** under "How many meals?". A whole day takes 2 to 6 meals. For the rest of the day it's "How many more meals?", 1 to 6, and the form says about how many calories are left.
2. Turn on **Only plan from my pantry** to keep every food to what's in stock in your pantry, with no other foods, recipes or saved meals. Off, the plan leans on your pantry and can add other foods.
3. Turn on **Mix it up** for more variety than usual today.
4. Under "Anything to include or avoid?", type what you want today, such as "No fish today. Oats for breakfast. Eating out at dinner." It's optional: your pantry, saved meals and the last 7 days are included on their own.
5. Tap **Plan the day** (or **Plan the rest**), which also shows the number of meals.

The **Foods to avoid** row on the form opens your list of allergies and foods you don't eat. They're left out of every plan, so there's no need to type them each time. See [Keep a food out of your plans](/help/your-food/foods-to-avoid/).

> [!NOTE] Planning a day that already has meals
>
> A whole-day plan replaces the meals you've planned, and the form says how many. Anything you've logged stays. **Plan the rest** keeps everything you've eaten or already planned and fills in around it.

## Look over the proposed plan

While the planner works, Today says "Planning your day." and who's planning. The × beside **Planning…** stops it.

The plan opens as "Proposed plan". At the top is who wrote it, such as "Planned by Claude", then the ring and bars for where the plan lands, and a sentence: "Lands in range. Accept it, and the day is planned." Under that, a line says how many foods were matched to foods you've used. "All 15 foods matched from foods you've used" means the numbers are real. "All N foods are AI estimates — match them after you accept." means they're the planner's guesses; see [Match AI estimates to real foods](/help/your-food/match-foods/).

## Change it before you accept

- Tap the `…` beside a meal, then **Swap for another** for a different meal in its place, or **Refine this meal…** to ask for a change in your own words, such as "swap rice for quinoa".
- Under "Work with the AI", type in **Refine the whole plan…** to change the whole day, then tap the arrow.

Refining needs Claude or Apple Intelligence. A plan built from your own foods can't be refined here; accept it and edit any meal on Today. If a change can't be made, the plan stays as it was and the app says why.

## Accept or discard

Tap **Accept the plan**, which shows the plan's calories. The meals go onto your day as planned meals, ready to log.

**Cancel** asks "Discard this plan?". **Discard plan** throws it away; nothing has been added to your day. **Keep reviewing** goes back to it.

## What every plan keeps to

Whoever writes the plan, Residual holds it to a plausible day before you see it:

- no more than 300 g of a protein food, such as meat or fish, in one meal;
- no more than 30 g of a fat, such as oil or butter, in one meal;
- no meal over half the day's calories.

Then the solver sizes every portion to land your targets. A food you size by the serving is planned in whole servings. See [Keep a pantry, and size foods by the serving](/help/your-food/pantry/).

## Set how you like to eat

Open `More → How you eat` to tell every planner the same things each time.

- Under "How you like to eat", write a standing note, such as "Big breakfast, light dinner. I cook on Sundays and eat leftovers." It's sent with every plan, revision and food suggestion. Keep it to food and meals; today's wishes go on the plan form.
- **Variety** sets how much plans change from day to day. **Familiar** leans on what you usually eat. **Balanced** doesn't repeat a main protein or a dish from the last 2 days. **Mix it up** reaches for foods you haven't had lately and tries a new idea a day. Any day can mix it up with the switch on the plan form.

Who writes the plan, and what each planner is sent, is in [Choose who plans](/help/planning/who-plans/).
