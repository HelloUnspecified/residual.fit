## The plans

The Residual Plus screen shows the plans as cards you swipe through: **Annual** first, then **Quarterly** and **Monthly**. Each card shows its price in your currency, what the longer plans come to a month, and its own button. Every plan has everything, and every plan has the same free trial. What each costs, and why, is on the [pricing page](/pricing/).

## Start the free trial

You see Residual Plus during setup, right after the health disclaimer. Later, it's at `More → Residual Plus`, where the row reads **Subscribe** until you have it.

1. Swipe to the plan you want.
2. Tap **Start my 14-day free trial** on its card.
3. Confirm with the App Store.

Everything unlocks at once. Under the cards, How your free trial works lays out the dates:

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Today | Everything unlocks. Nothing is charged today. |
| The day the trial ends | Your plan starts, and you're billed for the plan you picked, unless you cancel first. |
| Anytime | Cancel in Settings. Cancel at least a day before the trial ends and you're never charged. |

If you've had Residual Plus before, the App Store doesn't give the trial again. The screen says “Welcome back.”, each card's button reads **Subscribe**, and you're billed the day you subscribe.

## Manage or cancel

Apple handles the payment. Residual only learns whether your subscription is active, not your card or anything else about it.

To change plans or cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Or open `More → Residual Plus`: the row reads **Active**, and tapping it opens the same subscription settings without leaving Residual.

## On a new iPhone

1. Sign in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.
2. Install Residual and open it.
3. If it asks you to subscribe, tap the “…” button at the top of the screen, then **Restore purchases**.

With iCloud on, your days, foods and targets come back from iCloud too. See [Sync, export and delete your data](/help/data-and-settings/sync-export-delete/).

## The “…” menu

The button at the top right of the Residual Plus screen holds:

- **Restore purchases**, for a subscription you already have.
- **Export my data**, when the app is locked.
- **Terms of Use** and **Privacy Policy**.
- **What Residual costs, and why**, which opens the pricing page.

> [!NOTE] When the subscription or trial ends
>
> Residual locks until you subscribe again. Your data stays, on your iPhone and in iCloud. From the lock you can still restore a subscription and export everything you've logged. What to do is in [Locked out, or asked to update](/help/troubleshooting/locked-or-update-required/).

## Offline

Starting a trial and restoring both need the App Store. Offline, the Residual Plus screen says “You're offline. Connect to start your free trial or restore your subscription.” A subscription you already have is known on your iPhone, so the app opens without waiting on the network.
