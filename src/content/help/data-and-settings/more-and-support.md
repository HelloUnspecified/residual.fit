## What's in More

More is grouped by what you'd come for, most often first. Recipes, Meals and Pantry each have their own tab, so they aren't here.

| Section | Rows |
| --- | --- |
| Your food | **Food library**, everything search offers as your own foods. **Grocery list**, everything planned ahead, by aisle. |
| Your plan | **Daily targets**. **Foods to avoid**. **How you eat**, a standing note and how much variety you want. **AI planning**, which shows who plans for you. |
| Display | **Appearance**: Match iPhone, Light or Dark. **Gauges**, which faces of the ring you tap through. **Units**, what new foods start in. |
| (no heading) | **Help**, with the practice day and short answers. **Support & feedback**. |
| Account and data | **Residual Plus**. **iCloud sync**, which says On or Off. **Export to a spreadsheet**. |
| Share | **Share Residual**, a link to the app. **Newsletter & updates**. |
| About | **About Residual**. **Legal**: Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and Health disclaimer. **Rate Residual**. |

When a newer version is in the App Store, About also has a row that reads Update to Residual and the version number.

## Help in the app

`More → Help` starts with **Practice day**, a whole day to plan, log and land where nothing is saved. Under it are short answers in two groups, Your day and Your food, such as “Why did my meals change size?” and “How do I keep a food out of my plans?”. The [practice day](/help/getting-started/practice-day/) has its own article.

## Contact support

Open `More → Support & feedback`. Each of the three rows opens an email to support@residual.fit:

- **Get help**, when something isn't working or doesn't make sense.
- **Report a bug**, with room for what happened, what you expected and the steps to make it happen again.
- **Suggest a feature**, for something you'd like Residual to do.

The email already has your app version, iOS version and iPhone model in it, so we can reproduce what you saw. The same three show under This device on the screen. A screenshot helps. We read every message.

If your iPhone has no mail account set up, Residual says “No email account”. Tap **Copy address** and write to us from any email app.

Under Common questions, the same screen has short answers to what people ask most, from “As close as it gets” to sync between devices.

You can also write to support@residual.fit from anywhere, without the app.

## Newsletter & updates

`More → Newsletter & updates` signs you up for what's new in Residual, what's coming next, and tips for planning your days, a few emails a month at most.

1. Type your address under Your email.
2. Tap **Sign me up**.
3. Open the confirmation email and tap its link.

You're on the list once you tap that link. Every email after has an unsubscribe link. If it says “Couldn't sign you up right now. Check your connection and try again.”, do that.
